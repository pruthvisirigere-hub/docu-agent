# New Joiner Onboarding — Northwind Labs

## Your First Day

On your first day, come to the office at 9:00 AM and check in at reception. Your buddy will meet you there and walk you through the building. Your laptop and access badge will be ready at your desk.

## Accounts and Access

IT creates your email, Slack, and HR portal accounts before your first day. If anything is missing, raise a ticket with the IT helpdesk. You get access to the code repositories after your manager approves the request, usually within the first week.

## Your Buddy

Every new joiner is paired with a buddy for the first month. Your buddy answers day-to-day questions, introduces you to the team, and helps you with your first tasks. Meet your buddy at least twice a week during the first month.

## Probation

The probation period is 3 months. At the end of probation, your manager reviews your progress and confirms your role. During probation you get the same leave allowance and benefits as any other employee.
