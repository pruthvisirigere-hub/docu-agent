# Leave Policy — Northwind Labs

## Annual Leave

Every full-time employee gets 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees get a pro-rated amount based on their contracted hours. Annual leave is counted in whole days, and you can take it in blocks as short as half a day.

## Sick Leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. If you are sick for more than 3 consecutive days, please share a doctor's note with HR. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

## How to Request Leave

Submit your request in the HR portal at least 2 weeks before the start date for annual leave. Your manager approves requests within 3 working days. For urgent leave, message your manager directly and file the request in the portal on the same day.

## Carryover

You can carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next year. Any days above 5 expire on 31 January. Carried-over days must be used by 30 June of the following year.
