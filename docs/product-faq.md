# Product FAQ — Northwind Desk

## Pricing

Northwind Desk has three plans. Starter is $29 per month, Growth is $79 per month, and Business is $199 per month. All plans are billed monthly and you can cancel at any time from the billing page. Annual billing saves 20 percent on every plan.

## Warranty

Every Northwind Desk comes with a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or normal wear and tear. To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a photo of the issue.

## Shipping

Orders ship within 2 working days from our warehouse. Delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in the US and 7 to 10 working days internationally. Shipping is free for orders over $150.

## Returns

You can return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The product must be in its original packaging. Start a return from your account page, print the prepaid label, and drop the parcel at any courier point. Refunds are processed within 5 working days of the warehouse receiving the return.
